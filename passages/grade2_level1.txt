Max found a shiny red bicycle at the park.
It was leaning against an old oak tree.
"Wow!" he thought, "I wonder whose bike this is?"
He looked around, but no one was nearby.
Max knew he shouldn't take it, but it was the coolest bike he had ever seen.
He decided to wait for a little while to see if someone would come back for it.
