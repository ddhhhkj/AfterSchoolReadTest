Mia was building a magnificent sandcastle. It had tall towers and a wide moat.
She used a bright red bucket and a small blue shovel.
Suddenly, a playful wave rushed up the shore and tickled the edge of her moat!
Mia quickly dug the moat deeper to protect her castle.
Her brother, Leo, came over to help. "Let's build a wall!" he suggested.
Together, they made the sandcastle even stronger.
---QUESTIONS---
Q1: What was Mia building?
A) A toy boat
B) A sandcastle
C) A hole to China
Correct: B
Q2: What tools did Mia use?
A) A rake and a hoe
B) A red bucket and a blue shovel
C) A watering can and a cup
Correct: B
Q3: What almost ruined her sandcastle?
A) A big dog
B) Her brother Leo
C) A playful wave
Correct: C
Q4: How did Mia and Leo make the castle stronger?
A) They added more water
B) They built a wall and dug the moat deeper
C) They put shells on top
Correct: B
