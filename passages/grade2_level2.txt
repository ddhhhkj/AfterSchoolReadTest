Did you know that chameleons are amazing lizards? They can change their skin color!
This special skill is called camouflage. It helps them blend in with their surroundings, like leaves or branches.
This keeps them safe from predators, which are animals that might want to eat them.
Chameleons also have long, sticky tongues that they use to catch insects for food.
Their eyes can move in two different directions at the same time!
