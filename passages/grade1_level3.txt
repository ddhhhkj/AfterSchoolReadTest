Once, a little star was afraid of the dark.
"It's silly," said the Moon. "You ARE the dark, little star. You shine in it!"
The little star thought about this. It looked at all the other stars twinkling far away.
It took a deep breath and shone its tiny light. The dark didn't feel so scary anymore.
It felt like a big, cozy blanket.
---QUESTIONS---
Q1: Who was afraid of the dark?
A) The Moon
B) A little star
C) A fluffy cloud
Correct: B
Q2: What did the Moon tell the little star?
A) To hide until morning
B) That it shines in the dark
C) That the dark is scary
Correct: B
Q3: What did the other stars do?
A) They laughed at the little star
B) They hid from the dark
C) They were twinkling far away
Correct: C
Q4: How did the dark feel to the star at the end?
A) Cold and empty
B) Like a big, cozy blanket
C) Still very scary
Correct: B
