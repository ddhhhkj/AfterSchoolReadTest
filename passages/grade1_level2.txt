Sam has a blue kite. He likes to fly it at the park.
The wind helps the kite go up, up, up into the sky.
His dog, Max, barks at the kite. Max thinks it's a big bird!
Sam laughs and holds the string tight.
---QUESTIONS---
Q1: What color is Sam's kite?
A) Red
B) Blue
C) Green
Correct: B
Q2: Where does Sam like to fly his kite?

B) In his backyard
C) At the park
Correct: C
Q3: What does Max the dog think the kite is?
A) A cloud
B) A big bird
C) A floating toy
Correct: B
Q4: What does Sam hold tight?
A) Max's leash
B) A balloon
C) The kite string
Correct: C
