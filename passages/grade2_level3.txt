Every year, the town of Sunnybrook held a Great Vegetable Race.
Farmer Giles entered his enormous pumpkin, Penelope. He had grown it all summer.
Penny the Parsnip was fast, and Carl the Carrot was a strong contender.
But Penelope the Pumpkin, though slow at first, began to roll faster and faster down the hill.
She rumbled past Penny and zipped by Carl, crossing the finish line with a happy thud!
Farmer Giles cheered. Penelope was the champion!
