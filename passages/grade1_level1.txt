Lily the ladybug loved to explore. One sunny morning, she crawled onto a big, green leaf.
"This leaf is so smooth!" she thought.
Suddenly, a tiny drop of water, a dewdrop, landed right beside her. It sparkled like a diamond.
Lily looked at her reflection in the dewdrop. She saw her own red shell with black spots.
---QUESTIONS---
Q1: What kind of bug is Lily?
A) A bee
B) A ladybug
C) A spider
Correct: B
Q2: What did Lily crawl onto?
A) A red flower
B) A rough rock
C) A big, green leaf
Correct: C
Q3: What landed beside Lily on the leaf?
A) A crumb of bread
B) A dewdrop
C) Another ladybug
Correct: B
Q4: What did Lily see in the dewdrop?
A) A tiny fish
B) Her reflection
C) A scary shadow
Correct: B
